It was a pleasure to be involved in the organisation of the [ACM Seasonal School on Responsible AI](https://www.aston.ac.uk/research/eps/acaira/events/summer-school-2026), held at the Aston Centre for [Artificial Intelligence Research and Application](https://www.aston.ac.uk/research/eps/acaira) (ACAIRA) at [Aston University](https://www.aston.ac.uk/) from 14 to 18 September.   


During the event, I delivered a talk titled "_Distilling Opaque ML Models into Interpretable Equations with Symbolic Regression_." I also contributed to two panel discussions addressing current challenges in the development and deployment of responsible AI solutions. Thank you to [Alina Patelli](https://research.aston.ac.uk/en/persons/alina-patelli/) and [Aniko Ekart](https://research.aston.ac.uk/en/persons/aniko-ek%C3%A1rt) for the invitation to participate and for coordinating the event.


Further details regarding the seasonal school can be found here: https://www.aston.ac.uk/research/eps/acaira/events/summer-school-2026
